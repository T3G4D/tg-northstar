About Us

We are a group of four bakers dedicated to serving the best baked goods this city has ever tasted. Years ago, the four of us met in culinary school and became quick friends.
We all shared a dream of opening our own bakery and seeing smiling faces when customers ate our goods. It felt like destiny pulled us together to realize all of our dreams at once.

Sourcing Philosophy

All of our ingredients are locally sourced, for both freshness and to support our local farmers. We're firm believers that the best flavors are only possible with the best ingredients.

Staff Highlights

Featuring four culinary school graduates with a combined experience of 40 years of baking under our belts, everything we serve is our greatest masterpiece.
